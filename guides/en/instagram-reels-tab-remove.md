# Can you remove the Reels tab from Instagram's bottom bar? (iPhone, 2026)

Instagram has no setting that removes the Reels tab from the bottom bar. What Instagram has said about its tabs, what Following and Screen Time change, and how to hide the tab.

## Short answers

### Can you remove the Reels tab from Instagram?

Not in the Instagram app. Instagram has not announced a setting that removes or hides the Reels tab, and we found none as of October 2026. To use Instagram without it, open Instagram in an app that hides the tab, such as minulo, where hiding is free and DMs keep working.

### Can I move the Reels tab or change the tab order on Instagram?

We found no setting for that. Instagram has tested its own tab orders: in October 2025 Adam Mosseri said a small group in India could opt in to having Reels and DMs as the first two tabs, and opt right out again.

### Does the Following feed get rid of Reels?

No. Following, which you pick by tapping Instagram at the top of Home, shows posts from people you follow in time order, according to Meta. It changes Home only, and the Reels tab stays in the bottom bar.

### Can Screen Time block only the Reels tab?

No. Screen Time limits whole apps or app categories, not tabs inside an app. Limiting Instagram limits DMs too.

### How do I use Instagram without the Reels tab?

Open Instagram in minulo on iPhone. Hide the Reels tab and Hide Explore remove those tabs, and Every tab but DMs leaves only DMs and notifications. Hiding is free. The official Instagram app itself doesn't change.
