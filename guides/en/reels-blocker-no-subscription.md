# Is there a Reels blocker without a subscription? (iPhone, 2026)

Reels blockers you don't have to subscribe to: Nope is completely free, UNDOOMED sells a one-time option, and minulo hides Reels for free.

## Short answers

### Is there a Reels blocker with a one-time purchase?

Yes. UNDOOMED lists a $29.99 lifetime Pro, and Reelmute offers Premium as a lifetime purchase. minulo Pro is sold only as a monthly or yearly subscription. SocialFocus is a $4.99 paid app, with an optional subscription on top.

### Is there a completely free Reels blocker?

Nope is completely free with no in-app purchases, but it works only in Safari on instagram.com and youtube.com. minulo, Stillfeed and Reelmute hide Reels for free in their own app and charge only for extras.

### Do I need minulo Pro to hide Reels?

No. Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free with no time limit. Pro is for Sleep mode, blocks longer than 2 hours, Hard Mode and a daily limit.

### How much is minulo Pro?

In the US it is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year, and the yearly plan has a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. The App Store shows prices in your local currency.

### Do I have to pay to keep DMs?

Not in minulo, Nope, Stillfeed, Reelmute or Awhile: they all say DMs keep working while Reels are hidden, for free. A messages-only view is paid in some apps, but free in minulo.
