# Instagram on iPad: where Reels appear and what you can turn off (2026)

Instagram's iPad app opens to Reels and has no setting to turn Reels off. Where Reels show up on iPad, how to use Following, snooze suggested posts and set a limit.

## Short answers

### Why does Instagram on iPad open to Reels?

That is how Instagram designed the iPad app. When it announced the app, Instagram said that when you open it you drop into Reels. It is not a bug and not a test.

### Can I turn off Reels in the Instagram iPad app?

We could not find a setting for that in Instagram's help as of October 2026. You can switch to Following and choose Latest, snooze suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days, and set a daily time limit, but Reels stay in the app.

### How do I see only people I follow on Instagram for iPad?

Tap the Following icon on the left of Home and choose Latest. It shows posts and reels from accounts you follow, newest first. All also shows only accounts you follow, but in recommended order.

### Can I make Latest the default view in Following on iPad?

You can put it first. Tap the adjust icon at the top right of Following, drag Latest to the top and tap Done. This sets the order of the views, not the screen Instagram opens to.

### Does minulo work on iPad?

No. minulo is an iPhone app and has no iPad version. On iPhone it opens Instagram's website with the Reels tab and suggested posts hidden, and your DMs keep working. Hiding is free.
