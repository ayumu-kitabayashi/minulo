# Am I addicted to my phone? How to check with your own numbers

Check your phone use with Screen Time's daily average, pickups and notifications, plus the 10-item SAS-SV. What the scores mean and why it isn't a diagnosis.

## Short answers

### How do I know if I'm addicted to my phone?

There is no single test. Look at a week of Screen Time (daily average, pickups, top apps) and answer a validated questionnaire such as the 10-item SAS-SV. Its authors say it identifies risk rather than diagnosing addiction. If phone use is causing problems you can't fix on your own, talk to a doctor or counselor.

### How many phone pickups a day is normal?

There is no official normal number. Apple shows your pickups but doesn't set a target, and I haven't found a study that does. Compare your own weeks, and notice which app you open first after picking up.

### Where can I see my pickups on iPhone?

Open Settings > Screen Time > Daily Average, choose Week or Day, and scroll down to Pickups and Notifications.

### What score on the SAS-SV means high risk?

In the original 2013 paper, 31 or more for boys and 33 or more for girls, out of 60. The authors say the scale screens for risk and isn't designed to diagnose.

### What should I do if my screen time is too high?

Start with the app at the top of your list. If it is Instagram, YouTube or TikTok, removing only the endless feed (Reels, Shorts or For You) lets you keep your messages. On iPhone, minulo does this for free. Check Screen Time again after a week.
