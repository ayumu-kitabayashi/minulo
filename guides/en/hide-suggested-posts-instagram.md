# How to hide suggested posts on Instagram on iPhone (2026)

Instagram has no switch to turn off suggested posts for good. Snooze them for 30 days, use the Following feed, tap Not interested or reset suggested content, or hide them for free in minulo and keep your DMs.

## Short answers

### Can you turn off suggested posts on Instagram permanently?

We could not find a way in Instagram's help as of October 2026. The longest built-in option is snoozing all suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days, which you need to repeat when it ends. To keep them hidden, open Instagram in an app that hides suggested posts, such as minulo, where this is free.

### How do I snooze suggested posts on Instagram for 30 days?

Tap Options (the three dots) above a suggested post in your Feed, tap Not interested, then choose Snooze. All suggested posts in your Feed pause for 30 days.

### Does the Following feed get rid of suggested posts?

Yes, while you are in it. Tap the Instagram logo and choose Following to see only posts from accounts you follow, newest first, going back 30 days. The Reels tab and Explore are still there.

### Does resetting suggested content stop suggested posts?

No. Reset suggested content clears what Instagram has learned about you across Explore, Reels and Feed, but suggested posts keep appearing and become personalized again as you use the app. It cannot be undone.

### How do I hide suggested posts but keep my DMs?

iPhone Screen Time blocks the whole app, DMs included, so it does not help here. Use an app that opens Instagram with suggested posts hidden. In minulo, hiding suggested posts, Reels and Explore is free, and DMs keep working.
