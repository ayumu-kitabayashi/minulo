# How to stop watching Reels and Shorts while studying: keep your messages, lose the feed (iPhone, 2026)

Focus mode only mutes notifications and Screen Time blocks whole apps, DMs included. How to study without Reels and Shorts but keep group chats, on iPhone.

## Short answers

### How do I stop using my phone while studying?

The surest way is to put your phone in another room or a time-locking container, but then messages can't reach you either. If you need to stay reachable, mute notifications with a Focus, split your time with a focus timer such as Forest, and hide Reels and Shorts so a quick reply doesn't turn into scrolling.

### Is there an app that limits social media only during exam study?

To block whole apps, use iPhone Screen Time or Forest's in-session blocking (iOS 16 or later). If you want to keep DMs and group chats, an app that hides only Reels and Shorts, such as minulo or SocialLite, fits better.

### I set Screen Time, but I keep turning it off myself.

According to Apple, Screen Time limits can be ignored by default. Set a Screen Time passcode and have someone else, such as a family member, choose it, so you can't undo it yourself.

### Can I block Instagram Reels but keep iMessage and WhatsApp?

Screen Time can't block Reels inside Instagram. minulo opens Instagram with Reels and Explore hidden, and DMs keep working. Messaging apps you don't add to minulo's block list keep working as usual.
