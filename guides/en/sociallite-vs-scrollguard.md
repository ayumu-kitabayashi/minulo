# SocialLite vs ScrollGuard: how the two Reels blockers differ on iPhone (2026)

SocialLite is an app of its own that can block the official apps; ScrollGuard is a Safari extension with an Android version. Networks, prices and privacy labels compared, plus where minulo fits.

## Short answers

### Which is better, SocialLite or ScrollGuard?

It depends on where you want to scroll. SocialLite is an app of its own that can block the official apps and has push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat. ScrollGuard stays in Safari, lists TikTok and has an Android version. minulo is an app of its own that also lists TikTok, and hiding is free.

### Is ScrollGuard safe?

On the US App Store (checked 2026-11-01), ScrollGuard's privacy label lists only Data Not Linked to You, usage data for analytics. SocialLite's label lists Identifiers under Data Used to Track You. Apple notes that developers write these labels and Apple does not verify them.

### Does SocialLite block apps or work in a browser?

Its release notes say it can block native apps and redirect to SocialLite with custom shield screens. Its App Store description doesn't mention Safari. ScrollGuard is the one that works through Safari.

### Do SocialLite or ScrollGuard work with TikTok?

ScrollGuard's website lists TikTok for iPhone. SocialLite's App Store page doesn't mention TikTok. minulo opens TikTok with For You hidden while messages keep working, for free.

### Which one is cheaper?

Both are free to download. SocialLite's Pro costs $6.99 to $17.99 a month or $19.99 to $47.90 a year depending on the offer. ScrollGuard Pro is listed at $3.99 to $59.99 without a breakdown. In minulo, hiding is free and Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (US App Store).

### Can either one remove Reels from the official Instagram app?

No. On iPhone, ScrollGuard works through Safari and web apps, and SocialLite blocks the official app and sends you to its own view. minulo works the same way as SocialLite here.
