# Why Instagram opens to Reels, and how to get your feed back (2026)

Instagram is testing an option to open to Reels, and its iPad app opens there. How to get back to your feed and open Instagram to DMs instead.

## Short answers

### Why does my Instagram open to Reels now?

Instagram has been testing Reels-first layouts. In October 2025 it let a small group in India make Reels and DMs the first two tabs, and in October 2026 it was reported testing an option to open straight to the Reels feed. Both were described as optional. On iPad, the Instagram app opens to Reels by design.

### How do I make Instagram open to my feed again?

If you opted into a Reels-first test, switch back; Instagram said you can leave these tests, but we could not confirm the exact menu in its help. Otherwise tap Home to get to your feed, and tap Instagram at the top left › Following to see only people you follow. Instagram doesn't remember Following, so you choose it again each time.

### Can I remove the Reels tab from Instagram?

No. We could not find a setting in Instagram's help that removes the Reels tab as of October 2026. To use Instagram without it, open Instagram in an app that hides the Reels tab, such as minulo, where this is free and DMs keep working.

### Why does Instagram on iPad open to Reels?

Instagram designed its iPad app that way. When it launched in September 2025, Instagram said that when you open the app you drop into Reels, with Stories at the top and messages one tap away. The iPad app also has a Following tab with All, Friends and Latest views.

### How do I open Instagram straight to DMs?

We found no setting in the Instagram app for this. In minulo, Messages opens straight from the minulo home screen, and you can turn on Every tab but DMs so only DMs and notifications are left. Hiding is free.
