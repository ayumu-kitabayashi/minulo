Brain rot is Oxford's 2024 Word of the Year. Studies link heavy short video use with poorer attention. Hide Reels and Shorts on iPhone and keep DMs.

## Short answers

### Is brain rot real?

It is a slang term, not a diagnosis. Oxford defines it as the supposed deterioration of a person's mental state from too much trivial online content. Studies link heavy short video use with poorer attention but haven't proven cause.

### How long does it take to recover from brain rot?

There is no medical timeline for it. In one 2025 experiment, people whose mobile internet was blocked for two weeks showed better sustained attention, but that blocked all mobile internet, not only short videos.

### Do I have to delete Instagram and TikTok?

No. minulo hides Reels, Shorts and TikTok For You inside its own view, so you can keep DMs, your following feed and search.

### Does resetting the algorithm fix it?

Resetting clears what the app suggests, but the feed fills up again as you watch. Hiding the feed removes the place where the clips appear.
