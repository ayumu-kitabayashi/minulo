# How to hide the Explore page on Instagram on iPhone (2026)

Instagram has no setting to remove the Explore tab. How to change what it shows with Not interested, Sensitive content or a reset, and how to hide it free.

## Short answers

### Can you remove the Explore page on Instagram?

Not with a setting. As of October 2026 we could not find one in Instagram's help. You can change what Explore shows, but the tab stays. To hide it, open Instagram in an app that hides Explore, such as minulo, where this is free and DMs keep working.

### How do I clean up my Instagram Explore page?

In Explore, touch and hold a post you don't want and tap Not interested. You can also set Sensitive content to Less under Content preferences, or use Reset suggested content to start your recommendations over. A reset cannot be undone.

### Does resetting suggested content clear Explore?

It clears your recommendations across Explore, Reels and Feed at the same time, so Explore starts fresh. The tab itself stays, and Instagram's help says suggestions become personalized again as you like, share and interact with content.

### Can Screen Time block only Explore on Instagram?

No. Screen Time limits or blocks the whole Instagram app. It has no option for one tab inside the app, and blocking Instagram this way blocks your DMs too.

### How do I hide Explore but keep my DMs?

Use an app that opens Instagram with Explore hidden. In minulo, turn on Reels and Explore tabs in the Instagram settings. It is free, it stays on until you turn it off, and DMs, posts from people you follow and search keep working.
