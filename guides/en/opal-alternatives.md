# Opal alternatives that still let you use Instagram DMs (2026)

Opal alternatives that keep Instagram DMs: Opal, Brick and Screen Time block the whole app. minulo hides only Reels and Shorts so DMs still work.

## Short answers

### Is Opal free?

Opal is free to download, but most features sit behind a subscription. On the US App Store (October 2026) its in-app purchases include a yearly plan at $99.99, monthly plans at $19.99 and weekly plans from $4.99.

### What are free alternatives to Opal?

iPhone Screen Time is built in and free, but it limits whole apps. ScreenZen's only in-app purchases are optional tips, so its features are free to use. one sec is free for one app; one sec pro is $6.99 a month or $19.99 a year in the US. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free on all 10 networks, and only schedules, Hard Mode and multiple block lists are paid.

### Opal, one sec or ScreenZen: which should I pick?

Pick by what you want to happen when you reach for the app. Opal and Brick block it; one sec and ScreenZen make you wait first; minulo opens it without Reels and Shorts, so you can still read and send DMs. If the problem is Reels rather than Instagram as a whole, removing Reels is the gentler option.

### What is a good Opal alternative if I still need Instagram DMs?

Apps that hide only Reels and Shorts leave DMs working. minulo shows Instagram and 9 other networks with Reels, Shorts and suggested posts hidden, and it can lock the official Instagram app so you open minulo instead. Hiding is free.

### Does Opal block Instagram DMs?

Opal blocks the apps you choose, so while Instagram is blocked you cannot open it at all, which includes your DMs. Opal's description talks about app limits and app blockers; it does not describe hiding parts of an app.

### Is a pause app like one sec better than blocking?

one sec says it delays access instead of blocking, which it describes as psychologically different, and reports that app usage drops by 57% on average. After the pause the whole app opens, Reels included. minulo removes Reels and Shorts instead, so what you open is already without them.
