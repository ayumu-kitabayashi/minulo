# How to use Snapchat only for chats and friends’ Stories (and what you can’t hide)

Snapchat has no chats and Stories only mode. Watch friends’ Stories from the Chat screen, trim Discover, and see what Snapchat says can’t be turned off.

## Short answers

### Can I use Snapchat only for chats and friends’ Stories?

Not with a Snapchat setting. You can watch friends' Stories from the circles on the Chat screen so you rarely open Stories or Spotlight. To have Spotlight and Discover hidden, open Snapchat in minulo, where this is free.

### Can I turn off Discover on Snapchat?

No. Snapchat's help says you can't completely disable the Discover section. You can press and hold a Story or Show and tap Hide this Content, one item at a time, and unfollow accounts in Manage Accounts I Follow.

### Can I remove Spotlight from Snapchat?

We found no setting for it in Snapchat's help. Snapchat+ subscribers can change the Home Tab the app opens on, but Spotlight stays in the tab bar.

### Why does my Snapchat have fewer tabs?

Snapchat says feature availability can vary by region, age and when you created your account, so some people see a version with fewer tabs. Chatting with friends and viewing friend Stories still work in it.

### How do I mute a friend’s Story on Snapchat?

Swipe right from the Camera to the Chat screen, press and hold the friend's name, tap Story Settings and turn on Mute Story. Their Story stops appearing near the top, and they are not told.
