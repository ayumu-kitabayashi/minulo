# How to use Facebook only for family and friends, without Reels (iPhone, 2026)

Facebook has no switch for Reels. The Feeds tab lists recent posts from people and groups you follow. minulo hides Reels and Watch for free and keeps Messages.

## Short answers

### Can I use Facebook only for family and friends without Reels?

Yes, with minulo, which opens Facebook with Reels and Watch hidden for free and keeps Notifications, Messages, Friends and Feed. The official app has no switch for Reels, but its Feeds tab shows recent posts from the people and groups you follow.

### How do I see only posts from people I follow on Facebook?

Open the Feeds tab: tap Menu, scroll down and tap Feeds. Facebook says it lists the most recent posts from the people, Favorites, Pages and groups you follow. It switches back to the ranked Feed when you reopen the app.

### Can I turn off Reels on Facebook?

We couldn't find a setting for that in Facebook's help. It says reels may appear in your Feed and in Video, and that all videos on Facebook are now reels. You can mark reels Not Interested, or hide them with minulo.

### How do I stop seeing someone's posts for a while?

Tap Options on one of their posts and choose Snooze for 30 days. Facebook says you won't see their posts in your Feed for 30 days. You can end it early under Settings, Content preferences, Snooze.

### Will family messages still reach me if I block the Facebook app?

While minulo blocks the official Facebook app, its notifications don't arrive. You can still read Messages inside minulo. Apps you don't choose to block, such as Messenger, stay open.

### Is minulo free for Facebook?

Hiding Reels and Watch, the Home feed and Marketplace is free, and blocking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. Longer blocks, schedules by day and time, Hard Mode and multiple block lists are part of minulo Pro.
