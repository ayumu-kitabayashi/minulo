# How to hide YouTube comments on iPhone (2026)

YouTube's comment settings are for creators, and we found no switch for viewers. minulo hides YouTube comments on iPhone for free, and Shorts too if you like.

## Short answers

### Can you turn off comments on YouTube as a viewer?

We found no such setting in YouTube Help. Its comment settings are for creators managing their own videos. minulo hides comments on iPhone for free when you open YouTube inside it.

### Does hiding comments in minulo hide them for everyone?

No. They are hidden only on your screen inside minulo. Other viewers still see them, and the creator's settings don't change.

### Can I read comments again later?

Yes. Turn Comments off in the gear menu next to YouTube. You can change what is hidden at any time.

### Can I hide Shorts at the same time?

Yes. Shorts, Home recommendations and Everything on home but search are switches in the same menu, and hiding them is free too.

### Does it hide comments in the official YouTube app?

No. minulo can't change the official app. If you want to stay out of it, lock it in the Block tab, free for up to 2 hours per block.

### Is it free?

Yes. Hiding comments, Shorts and Home recommendations is free with no time limit. Schedules, longer blocks and Hard Mode are in minulo Pro.
