# What happens to YouTube Home when you turn off watch history (iPhone, 2026)

With watch history off and no recent history, YouTube removes Home recommendations and hides the feeds bar. How to pause history and turn it back on.

## Short answers

### Does turning off YouTube watch history remove Home recommendations?

It can. YouTube's help says Home recommendations are removed when you have no relevant or significant recent watch history and have said you don't want recommendations based on it. If you still have recent history, delete it as well as pausing.

### Why is my YouTube Home page empty?

If your watch history is paused and there is little or no recent history, YouTube removes recommendations from Home. YouTube's help says a new user who hasn't watched any videos yet sees the same thing.

### Where did the Subs feed go after I paused my history?

YouTube's help on feeds says the feeds and the top bar are not visible while watch history is paused or turned off. Subs is one of those feeds. The help page points you to turning watch history back on.

### How do I turn YouTube watch history back on?

In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture, then Settings, Manage all history, and the Controls tab. Select "Include the YouTube videos you watch" again. Home shows recommendations again once you start watching videos.

### Can I keep my watch history and still hide Home recommendations?

Not with a YouTube setting we could find. In minulo you can hide Home recommendations and Shorts while your history stays on, and Subscriptions, Watch later and search keep working. It's free.
