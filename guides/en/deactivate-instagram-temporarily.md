# How to temporarily deactivate Instagram on iPhone, and when blocking is better (2026)

Deactivate Instagram in Accounts Center to hide your profile, once a week. Logging back in reactivates it, so it doesn't work as a break if you need DMs.

## Short answers

### How do I temporarily deactivate my Instagram?

On iPhone, go to Profile › Menu › Accounts Center › Manage account, tap Manage next to your profile, then Deactivation or deletion › Deactivate account › Continue. If your Accounts Center shows Accounts at the bottom, the path is Personal details › Account ownership and control › Deactivation or deletion.

### What happens when you deactivate Instagram?

Your profile, photos, comments and likes are hidden until you reactivate by logging back in. Nothing is deleted.

### How long can I deactivate Instagram for?

Instagram's help gives no time limit. The account stays deactivated until you log back in.

### Can I still use DMs if I deactivate Instagram?

Not without ending the break. Logging back in reactivates the account. To keep DMs and stop Reels, block or hide Reels instead, for example with minulo.

### How often can I deactivate Instagram?

Once a week, according to Instagram's help.
