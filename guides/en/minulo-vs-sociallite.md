# minulo vs SocialLite: two apps that hide Reels and keep your DMs (2026)

minulo vs SocialLite: both hide Reels and Shorts and keep DMs. SocialLite adds push notifications; minulo covers 10 services and hiding is free.

## Short answers

### What is the difference between minulo and SocialLite?

They work in a very similar way: both open social media in their own view, hide Reels and recommendations, and keep DMs. The differences are how many services they cover (minulo has 10, including TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and LinkedIn), notifications (SocialLite supports push for Instagram and Snapchat), use beyond the iPhone (minulo has a Mac app and a Chrome extension) and track record (SocialLite has far more ratings).

### Which one should I choose?

SocialLite suits you if you don't want to miss Instagram notifications or you value a long list of ratings. minulo suits you if you also want TikTok or Threads cleaned up, want the same thing on your computer, or want the official apps locked so you land in minulo instead. Both are free to try.

### Is minulo free?

Hiding Reels, Shorts and recommended posts is free, and so is locking the official apps. Scheduled blocking and Hard Mode are part of Pro. SocialLite is also free to download with in-app purchases; its App Store description doesn't say which features are free.

### Is it safe to sign in?

With minulo you sign in on each service's official page, so minulo never sees your password. SocialLite says it stores your information on your device and does not send it to its servers.

### How much does SocialLite cost?

SocialLite is free to download. On the US App Store (checked 2026-10-26), Pro costs $6.99 to $17.99 a month or $19.99 to $47.90 a year, depending on the offer shown, and a Family plan is $12.99 a month. For comparison, minulo's Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US, and hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts in minulo is free.

### Is SocialLite good? What do the reviews say?

It is the most established app of its kind: more than 14,000 ratings on the US App Store with an average of 4.8 stars (October 2026). If you mainly use Instagram and want notifications while Reels are hidden, it is a safe pick. People usually look for an alternative when they also want TikTok, Threads, Pinterest or LinkedIn covered, or want hiding to stay free.
