# Is there Instagram Lite for iPhone? What to use instead (2026)

Instagram Lite is on Google Play for Android, and we found no Instagram Lite on the iPhone App Store. For a simpler Instagram without Reels, minulo hides Reels and Explore for free.

## Short answers

### Is Instagram Lite available on iPhone?

We found no Instagram Lite when we searched the App Store in the US, Japan, Korea and India on 2026-11-01. Google Play lists it for Android. On iPhone, minulo opens Instagram with Reels and Explore hidden while DMs keep working, and hiding is free.

### What is the simplest way to use Instagram on iPhone?

instagram.com in Safari needs no app, but it still has Reels and Explore. minulo opens the same website with Reels, Explore, suggested posts or Stories hidden, or with every tab but DMs hidden.

### Does minulo use less data than the Instagram app?

We haven't measured it, so we don't claim it. minulo is for hiding Reels and feeds, not for saving data.

### Can I use Instagram only for DMs on iPhone?

Yes. In minulo, turn on Every tab but DMs for Instagram. It is free, and only your messages and the notifications tab stay.

### Is minulo made by Instagram or Meta?

No. minulo is made by an independent developer, and you sign in on Instagram's own login page inside the app.

### Does minulo change the official Instagram app?

No. Reels are hidden only inside minulo. To stay out of the official app, lock it in the Block tab, free for up to 2 hours per block.
