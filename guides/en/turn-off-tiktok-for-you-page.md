# How to turn off the TikTok For You page on iPhone (and keep your DMs)

TikTok has no setting to remove For You. What Refresh, Not interested, keyword filters and the daily limit do, and how to hide For You but keep DMs.

## Short answers

### Can you turn off the For You page on TikTok?

We could not find a way in TikTok's help as of October 2026. For You is the first feed you see when you open TikTok, and TikTok's tools (Refresh your For You feed, Not interested, Filter keywords and Manage topics) change what it shows without removing it. To use TikTok without For You, open it in an app that hides it, such as minulo, where this is free.

### What does Refresh your For You feed do?

It starts your For You recommendations over. TikTok shows you some popular posts, and what you like and comment on shapes the feed from there. It only affects For You, not your Following feed, profile, inbox or ads, and it can't be undone. You find it under Profile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Content preferences.

### Can I use TikTok with only the Following feed?

Not in the TikTok app. It still opens on For You, and when you run out of posts in Following or the Friends Tab, TikTok suggests going to For You. In minulo you can hide For You, and Messages and Following open straight from its home screen.

### Does TikTok's daily screen time limit stop you from scrolling?

Not by itself. When you reach the limit, TikTok notifies you and you can close the app or type the passcode shown on the screen to keep watching, so it works as a reminder. For users aged 13 to 17 it is on by default at 1 hour.

### How do I stop scrolling TikTok but keep my DMs?

Limiting the TikTok app with iPhone Screen Time blocks your messages too. Instead, open TikTok in an app that hides For You and keeps messages, such as minulo. Hiding is free, and minulo can also lock the official TikTok app for up to 2 hours per block for free, so tapping its icon sends you to minulo.
