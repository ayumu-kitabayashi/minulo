# How to get YouTube notifications only from your subscriptions (iPhone, 2026)

Turn off Recommended videos in YouTube notification settings to stop alerts from channels you don't subscribe to, then set each channel's bell to All, Personalized or None.

## Short answers

### How do I stop YouTube notifications from channels I don't subscribe to?

In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture, then Settings and Notifications, and turn off Recommended videos. YouTube's help describes these as recommended video notifications from channels you are not subscribed to.

### What is the difference between All and Personalized on a YouTube channel?

All sends notifications for long-form uploads and live streams, and may include some personalized Shorts notifications. Personalized sends notifications for some uploads, Shorts and live streams, chosen using signals like your watch history and how often you watch the channel.

### Why am I not getting notifications for every upload?

YouTube's help says not every new upload results in a notification. If a channel is set to All but you haven't watched it for a while, its notifications only appear inside the app under the bell on Home. The Subs feed shows all recent uploads from your subscriptions.

### Can I change notifications for all my subscriptions at once?

There is a single list, but each channel has its own bell. Tap Subs in the top bar of the Home tab, tap All at the top right, then tap the bell next to each channel.

### Does minulo send YouTube notifications?

No. minulo does not send notifications for YouTube. While minulo has the official YouTube app locked, that app's notifications don't arrive either.

### Can I turn off only Shorts notifications on YouTube?

We couldn't find a Shorts-only switch in YouTube Help. For each channel you choose All, Personalized or None: Personalized includes some Shorts, All can also bring some personalized Shorts notifications, and None turns off everything from that channel. Turning off Recommended videos under Settings, Notifications stops notifications from channels you don't subscribe to.
