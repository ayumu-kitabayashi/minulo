# How to open Instagram straight to your DMs on iPhone, without the feed (2026)

Ways to open Instagram on your DMs instead of the feed on iPhone: message notifications, the icon's Quick Actions menu, messages-only notifications, and minulo's free DMs-only view.

## Short answers

### Can I make Instagram open straight to my DMs?

Yes, with minulo: its free Every tab but DMs setting opens Instagram with only DMs and the notifications tab left. Instagram itself has no such setting. Tapping a message notification also opens that chat, but the feed and Reels are still in the app.

### Does holding the Instagram icon open DMs?

Only if a messages item appears in its Quick Actions menu. Instagram decides what is in that menu, and we couldn't confirm the current list: a 2015 report mentions a direct message shortcut, and a later guide lists Switch Accounts, Camera, New Post and View Activity.

### Can I reply to an Instagram DM from the Lock Screen?

Apple says touching and holding a notification shows quick actions if the app offers them. We couldn't confirm that Instagram offers a reply there. If it doesn't, tapping the notification opens the chat.

### How do I get Instagram notifications only for DMs?

Go to Profile › Menu › Notifications, and below Pause push notifications turn off each kind that isn't about messages. Leave messages on and keep Instagram allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications.

### Will I still get DM notifications if I use minulo?

Yes, from the official Instagram app, as long as minulo isn't locking it. While minulo locks the official app, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own.
