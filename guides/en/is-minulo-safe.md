# Is minulo safe? What it can see, what it can't, and its limits (2026)

Is minulo safe? No account, and your social media password goes to Instagram or YouTube, never to minulo. What it sends, what Screen Time lets it see, and its limits.

## Short answers

### Is minulo safe?

minulo has no account and never receives your social media ID or password: you sign in on each network's own website inside the app, and the connection does not go through a minulo server. It sends only anonymous usage data and error reports to PostHog, tied to a random ID rather than to you. Your records stay on your iPhone, and deleting the app erases them. The only exceptions are your "Refer a friend" referral code with its passphrase and a marker for people who were already using minulo for free, which stay in the iPhone Keychain.

### Is minulo legit?

Yes. minulo is on the App Store (seller: ayumu kitabayashi) and is made by ReliefNote, a sole proprietorship in Hokkaido, Japan, founded in July 2025 by Ayumu Kitabayashi. Hiding Reels and Shorts is free. It is a new app (September 2026), so it has few ratings so far.

### Can minulo see my Instagram password?

No. You type your password on Instagram's own sign-in page shown inside minulo, and it is sent straight to Instagram. minulo does not receive, store or send your ID or password. Login cookies are kept in the storage iOS gives minulo, which other apps and the developer can't read.

### Does minulo collect my data or track me?

It collects anonymous usage data (actions like starting a block, which network you turned back from, language and region settings, iOS version), error reports with URLs and names removed, and whether you came from an App Store search ad. It does not collect your name, email, location or advertising identifier, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Its App Store label lists only Data Not Linked to You.

### Can minulo see which apps I block with Screen Time?

No. Apple gives apps opaque tokens for the apps you pick, to protect your privacy. minulo stores those tokens on your iPhone and passes them back to Screen Time, but it can't tell which apps they stand for.
