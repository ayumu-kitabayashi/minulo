# Safari extensions that hide Reels and Shorts on iPhone, compared (2026)

Nope, ScrollGuard, #blockit and YouTube-only Shorts blockers for Safari on iPhone: sites, price, iOS and what each hides, from the App Store. Plus minulo, free, outside Safari.

## Short answers

### Which Safari extension hides Reels and Shorts on iPhone?

minulo is a free iPhone app that hides Reels and Shorts inside its own app rather than in Safari. Among Safari extensions, per their App Store pages on 2026-10-26, Nope is free for instagram.com and youtube.com, ScrollGuard has Pro purchases and needs iOS 18, and #blockit covers six sites with a subscription after a 7-day free trial.

### Is there a free Safari extension that hides Instagram Reels?

Yes. Nope's App Store page says it is free with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads, and it can turn off the Reels page on instagram.com in Safari. It needs iOS 17 and had 2 US ratings on 2026-10-26.

### Do Safari extensions remove Reels from the Instagram app?

No. They work only on websites you open in Safari. No Shorts and Shorts Blocker for YouTube say on their App Store pages that the YouTube app is not affected.

### Which Safari extensions hide only YouTube Shorts?

Shorts Blocker for YouTube ($1.99), No Shorts: Blocker for YouTube ($2.99, one-time) and BlockYT for Safari ($2.99) work on YouTube only, per their US App Store pages on 2026-10-26.

### What is Short Video Hider?

We couldn't find an app by that name in the US App Store on 2026-10-26, so we couldn't confirm what it does or what it costs.

### Can I hide Reels and Shorts outside Safari?

Not with a Safari extension. minulo hides them in its own app and can lock the official apps with Screen Time, free for up to 2 hours per block. It can't change the official apps themselves.
