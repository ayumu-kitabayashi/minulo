# Can you set a time limit on Snapchat Spotlight? (Family Center and Screen Time, 2026)

Snapchat has no time limit for Spotlight alone. What Family Center shows parents, what iPhone Screen Time can limit, and how to use Snapchat without Spotlight.

## Short answers

### Can you set a time limit on Snapchat Spotlight?

Not as far as Snapchat's help shows in October 2026. There is no Spotlight timer for yourself, and Family Center shows a teen's time without capping it. iPhone Screen Time can limit the whole Snapchat app, chats included.

### Can parents see how much time a teen spends on Spotlight?

Partly. Family Center's Time Spent groups time into Messaging, Camera, Spotlight & Stories, and Map, with a daily average for the last seven days. Spotlight is counted together with Stories.

### Can Family Center block Spotlight?

No. Hide Sensitive Content in Family Center limits sensitive or suggestive content in places like Stories and Spotlight, but Spotlight stays in the app.

### Can Screen Time limit only Spotlight?

No. Screen Time limits the Snapchat app as a whole, so chats stop at the limit too. To keep chats without Spotlight, open Snapchat in an app that hides it, such as minulo.

### Who can use Snapchat Family Center?

Snapchat says parents and caregivers must be 18 or older and teens 13 to 17. The teen has to accept an invitation, and Family Center may not be available in every country yet.
