# How to block only Instagram during Downtime and keep LINE (iPhone, 2026)

Downtime blocks every app that isn't Always Allowed, so add LINE there and leave Instagram out. Or limit Instagram alone, or hide Reels with minulo for free.

## Short answers

### Can I block only Instagram with Downtime and keep LINE?

Yes, and minulo can do it for free too: it locks only the official Instagram app with Screen Time for up to 2 hours per block while LINE stays open. With Downtime, add LINE to Always Allowed, keep Instagram off that list and turn on Block at Downtime.

### Why does Downtime block LINE as well?

Apple says that during downtime only calls, messages and apps you choose to allow are available. LINE is a separate app, so it is blocked unless you add it to Always Allowed.

### Where is Always Allowed on iPhone?

Settings, Screen Time, Always Allowed. On iOS 26, add apps under Choose Apps. On iOS 27, tap Add Apps. Apps there stay available during downtime and schedules.

### Can App Limits block only Instagram at night?

App Limits can target only Instagram, but Apple's steps set an amount of time per day, not a time of night. By default the limit can also be ignored once reached.

### Does Downtime block Instagram DMs too?

Yes. Downtime works on the whole app, so DMs stop along with Reels. To keep DMs and drop Reels, open Instagram in minulo, which hides Reels for free.

### How does this work on iOS 27?

Use Screen Time Schedule and choose which Time Allowance categories each time window allows. We couldn't confirm which category LINE and Instagram are in, so add LINE to Always Allowed. All your Apple devices need iOS 27 or later.
