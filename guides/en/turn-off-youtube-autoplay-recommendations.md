# How to turn off YouTube autoplay and recommendations on iPhone

Turn off YouTube autoplay on iPhone (profile picture, Settings, Autoplay) and remove Home recommendations by pausing and deleting watch history. Or hide recommendations and Shorts in minulo for free.

## Short answers

### How do I turn off autoplay on YouTube on iPhone?

In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture, tap Settings at the top right, tap Autoplay and switch it off. You can also use the Autoplay switch at the top of the video player.

### How do I turn off recommendations on the YouTube homepage?

YouTube's help says that if you don't want video recommendations on Home, you can delete and turn off your watch history. To pause it on iPhone, tap your profile picture, then Settings, Manage all history, the Controls tab, and unselect "Include the YouTube videos you watch." Home recommendations are removed when you have no relevant recent watch history and have said you don't want recommendations based on it.

### Why does YouTube still autoplay after I turned it off?

Autoplay is set separately on each device. Turning it off on your computer doesn't turn it off in the YouTube app on your iPhone, so check Settings, then Autoplay, in the app itself.

### How do I stop YouTube from playing another video at the end?

Turn off Autoplay, or tap Cancel at the end of a video while Autoplay is on. YouTube's help also says the next video won't play automatically if you scroll past the video player or start typing, for example a comment or a search.

### Can I hide YouTube recommendations without deleting my watch history?

The way YouTube's help gives for removing Home recommendations is deleting and turning off watch history. Not interested and Don't recommend channel only change what gets recommended. If you want to keep your history, minulo opens YouTube with Home recommendations hidden while Subscriptions, Watch later and search still work. It's free.
