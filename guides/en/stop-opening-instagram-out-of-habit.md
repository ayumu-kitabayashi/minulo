# How to stop opening Instagram out of habit on iPhone, without deleting it (2026)

Your thumb opens Instagram before you decide to. Use iPhone Screen Time so the Instagram icon leads to a Reels-free Instagram, and keep your DMs.

## Short answers

### Do I have to delete Instagram?

No. The official app stays installed; minulo locks it with Screen Time and sends you to its Reels-free view.

### Can I still get to my DMs quickly?

Yes. Tap Open in minulo on the block screen; your DMs and group chats work there.

### Is this the same as an app limit?

No. An app limit blocks all of Instagram once time is up. minulo removes only the endless parts and keeps the rest usable.
