# Jomo alternatives that hide Reels and Shorts but keep your DMs (2026)

Jomo blocks apps, websites and categories with locks and Strict Mode. minulo hides Reels and Shorts so Instagram DMs still work. Hiding is free.

## Short answers

### Is Jomo free?

Jomo is free to download. Its US App Store page lists Jomo Plus at $5.99 a month or $29.99 a year, plus lifetime options. Check the page for what the free version includes.

### Does Jomo block Instagram DMs?

Jomo blocks the apps you choose, so when Instagram is blocked, its DMs are too. minulo hides Reels and suggested posts and keeps DMs working inside its own view.

### Can Jomo hide only Instagram Reels?

Its App Store page describes blocking apps, websites and categories, not hiding one part of an app. minulo hides Reels, Explore and suggested posts one item at a time.

### Can I use Jomo and minulo together?

Both use iPhone Screen Time to block apps. We haven't tested them side by side, so if you use both, check that the same app isn't blocked by each one at once.

### Is there a Jomo alternative that is free?

Apple's Screen Time App Limits and Downtime are built in. minulo is free to download, hides Reels and Shorts for free and locks the official apps for free, up to 2 hours per block.
