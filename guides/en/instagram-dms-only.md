# How to use Instagram for DMs only on iPhone (2026): keep group chats, lose Reels

Instagram has no messages-only mode. How to keep Instagram DMs and group chats on iPhone while hiding Reels, Explore and the feed, step by step.

## Short answers

### Does Instagram have a messages-only mode?

Not that we could find. Instagram lets adults switch to the Following feed or set a daily time reminder, but the Reels tab and Explore stay. Apps such as minulo add a DMs-only view on top of Instagram's website.

### Will I still get my group chats?

Yes. Group chats are part of Instagram DMs, and DMs keep working in minulo's DMs-only view.

### Do I have to delete the official Instagram app?

No. minulo can lock the official app with Screen Time and open its own DMs-only Instagram when you tap the Instagram icon.
