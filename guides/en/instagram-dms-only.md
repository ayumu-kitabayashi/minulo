# How to use Instagram only for DMs on iPhone (2026): keep group chats, lose Reels

Instagram has no messages-only mode. Keep DMs and group chats on iPhone and hide Reels, Explore and the feed with minulo's free "Every tab but DMs".

## Short answers

### How do I use Instagram only for DMs on iPhone?

Open Instagram in minulo and turn on the free "Every tab but DMs" setting. Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts are hidden, and DMs, group chats included, keep working. To stop the official app from pulling you back into Reels, let minulo lock it with Screen Time, free for up to 2 hours per block; tapping the Instagram icon then opens minulo's DMs-only Instagram instead. Instagram has no messages-only mode of its own, and iPhone Screen Time can only limit the whole app, DMs included.

### Can I use Instagram just for messaging, without Reels or the feed?

Yes, in minulo. Its "Every tab but DMs" setting for Instagram is free and leaves only DMs and the notifications tab. In most other apps with a messages-only view, that view is paid: Messages Only in UNDOOMED is Pro (per its website), DMs only in Stillfeed is Pro, and Reelmute needs Premium.

### Does Instagram have a messages-only mode?

Not that we could find, but minulo's free "Every tab but DMs" setting opens Instagram with only DMs left. Instagram itself lets adults switch to the Following feed or set a daily time reminder, but the Reels tab and Explore stay. Apps such as minulo add a DMs-only view on top of Instagram's website.

### Will I still get my group chats?

Yes. Group chats are part of Instagram DMs, and DMs keep working in minulo's DMs-only view.

### Do I have to delete the official Instagram app?

No. minulo can lock the official app with Screen Time and open its own DMs-only Instagram when you tap the Instagram icon.

### What happens when I open a Reel a friend sent in DMs?

Inside minulo, a Reel you open from a DM pauses before the vertical video feed starts. Tap Open for 5 min if you want to watch it; after five minutes, blocking turns back on by itself. In the Instagram app, a Reel opened from a chat plays in the Reels viewer, and one swipe takes you to the next one. So when you open a Reel to reply to a friend, the next one is not already playing. YouTube Shorts sent in a DM pause the same way.

### What if one group chat sends too many notifications?

Group chats work in minulo's DMs-only view, so you can read and reply there as usual. If one group is too loud, mute just that chat in the Instagram app: open the chat, tap the name at the top, then Mute, and turn on Mute messages. Instagram doesn't tell people you muted them. Your other chats keep notifying you.

### How do I get Instagram notifications only for messages?

In the Instagram app, tap your profile, then Menu at the top right, then Notifications. Below Pause push notifications, open each notification type that isn't about messages and turn it off, and leave messages on. We couldn't confirm the exact names of every type in Instagram's help, so go by what each one is about. Also check that Allow Notifications is on for Instagram in iPhone Settings › Notifications. minulo sends no notifications of its own, and while minulo blocks the official app, that app's notifications don't arrive either.

### Can I still see Stories if I keep only DMs?

With Every tab but DMs on, only DMs and notifications stay. If you want friends' Stories too, leave Every tab but DMs off and turn on Reels and Explore tabs and Suggested posts instead. Reels, Explore and suggested posts are hidden, Stories stay at the top of the feed, and DMs keep working. Posting Stories isn't available in minulo, because Instagram's website doesn't have it.

### Isn't the Following feed or snoozing suggested posts enough?

If posts from strangers are the only problem, Instagram's own settings help a little. Tap Instagram at the top left of your feed and choose Following: you get posts and reels from accounts you follow, newest first, with no suggested posts. Instagram doesn't remember this choice, so the next time you open the app you are back on the regular feed. You can also snooze suggested posts for 30 days from Options › Not interested on a suggested post, but they come back after 30 days, and Reels and Explore are not covered. Either way, the Reels tab and Explore stay on screen.

### Can I use Instagram DMs on a computer?

Yes. Open instagram.com in a browser, log in and click Messages on the left. About Instagram, Instagram's official site, describes clicking Compose, choosing people, then Chat and Send. The website has the Reels and Explore tabs too, so moving to a computer doesn't make Instagram DMs only. We couldn't confirm in Instagram Help which message features the computer website has.

### What can't minulo do?

Reels are hidden only inside minulo; the official Instagram app doesn't change. Voice messages, calls and posting Stories aren't available, because Instagram's website doesn't have them. While minulo blocks the official app, that app's notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. minulo is an iPhone app, with a Mac version for Safari and Chrome. There is no Android version. See what minulo can't do.
