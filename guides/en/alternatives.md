# Best iPhone apps to block Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts but keep your DMs (2026)

Best iPhone apps to block Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts but keep DMs: in-app browsers (minulo, SocialLite, UNDOOMED) and Safari extensions compared.

## Short answers

### Which one is the best?

It depends on what you need. SocialLite has by far the most ratings (14,384 in the US) and sends push notifications. If you only use Instagram, Awhile is simple, and Nope is completely free in Safari. If you want more networks, UNDOOMED and minulo cover the most; minulo also covers TikTok and Snapchat (10 networks in all), and hiding is free.

### What do the App Store ratings say?

On the US App Store (October 2026), SocialLite has 14,384 ratings, SocialFocus 325, ScrollGuard 116, UNDOOMED 107 and Dull 54; the others have fewer than 35. minulo is new and has no US ratings yet. The number of ratings shows how many people have tried an app, not whether it hides what you need, so check the networks and price columns too.

### Which app hides Instagram Reels but keeps DMs?

SocialLite, UNDOOMED, Awhile, LoomWeb, Nope and minulo all say DMs keep working while Reels are hidden. Awhile covers Instagram only; Nope covers Instagram and YouTube; UNDOOMED, LoomWeb and minulo cover several networks. minulo supports 10, including TikTok and Snapchat.

### Can any of these apps remove Reels inside the official Instagram app?

Only WallHabit says it can block parts of apps such as Reels and Shorts, and its description does not explain how. The other apps show Instagram in their own browser or in Safari rather than changing the official app. minulo takes a different route: it can lock the official Instagram app with Screen Time, and when you try to open it, it sends you to minulo, where Reels are hidden.

### Which option is completely free?

Nope says it is fully free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases, and covers Instagram and YouTube in Safari. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free on all 10 networks; only schedules, Hard Mode and multiple block lists are paid.

### What is a good SocialLite alternative?

SocialLite has by far the most ratings (14,384 in the US). If you want another app that keeps DMs and hides short videos, UNDOOMED and minulo cover the most networks. minulo also covers TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and LinkedIn and has a Chrome extension, but it is new and has no US ratings yet.

### Is minulo safe to log in with?

You log in on each network's official web page inside minulo. minulo does not receive or store your ID or password, and it has no account of its own. Usage analytics are anonymous and not linked to you.
