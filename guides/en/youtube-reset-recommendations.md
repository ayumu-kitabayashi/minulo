# How to reset your YouTube recommendations on iPhone (2026)

We found no single reset button in YouTube's help. It says you can start fresh by deleting watch and search history, then clear Not interested and Show fewer Shorts.

## Short answers

### Can you reset YouTube recommendations?

There is no single reset button that we could find in YouTube's help as of October 2026. The help says you can start fresh by deleting your watch and search history. Feedback such as Not interested and Show fewer Shorts is cleared separately in My Activity.

### Does deleting watch history reset my recommendations?

It removes what those videos told YouTube. The help says YouTube won't base future recommendations on deleted content. Recommendations build up again from what you watch next.

### How do I undo Not interested on YouTube?

Go to My Activity, open Other Google activity, find YouTube 'Not interested' feedback and select Delete. This also clears Don't recommend channel feedback.

### How do I get Shorts back after choosing Show fewer Shorts?

Go to My Activity, open Other activity, and under Show fewer Shorts select Delete.

### How do I stop YouTube recommendations completely?

YouTube's help says that if you don't want video recommendations on Home, you can delete and turn off your watch history. If you'd rather keep your history, minulo hides Home recommendations and Shorts for free.
