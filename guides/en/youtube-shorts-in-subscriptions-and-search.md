# Why YouTube Shorts still show in Subscriptions and search, and what you can do (iPhone, 2026)

YouTube says Shorts also show in search, Subscriptions, channel pages and notifications. Filter search by Type, and use minulo for free to hide the Shorts shelf and tab.

## Short answers

### Why do Shorts still show in Subscriptions after I set the Shorts limit to 0?

minulo hides the Shorts shelf and tab for free and opens Shorts links in the normal player, but YouTube's own limit is about the Shorts feed. YouTube's help says viewers also find Shorts in Subscriptions, search, channel pages and notifications.

### How do I remove Shorts from YouTube search results?

After searching, tap Filter and choose a Type. YouTube's help lists the Type filter but not its choices, and we couldn't confirm that it stays on for the next search.

### Can I hide Shorts in the YouTube Subscriptions feed?

We couldn't find a setting in YouTube's help that hides Shorts in Subscriptions. In minulo, a Short you tap there opens in the normal player instead of the swipe feed.

### Does the Shorts feed limit stop Shorts I open from search?

YouTube's help only says a reminder appears when the limit is reached in the Shorts feed, with an option to dismiss it. It doesn't say what happens to Shorts opened from search.

### Does minulo remove every Short from Subscriptions and search?

No, we don't promise that. It hides the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab and opens Shorts links in the normal player, only inside minulo. The official YouTube app doesn't change.

### Do these settings work in the official YouTube app?

The Shorts feed limit and the search filter are in the YouTube app. minulo's settings work only when you open YouTube inside minulo.
