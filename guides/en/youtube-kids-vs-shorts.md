# YouTube Shorts feed limit on teen, kid and adult accounts: what YouTube's help says (2026)

How YouTube's Shorts feed limit differs on your own account, a supervised teen account, a supervised kid account and YouTube Kids, based on YouTube Help.

## Short answers

### Can I ignore the YouTube Shorts feed limit?

On your own account, yes. YouTube's help says that when you reach the limit, a reminder appears and you can dismiss or ignore your set limit.

### Can parents set a Shorts limit on a teen's YouTube account?

Yes, with a supervised teen account. A linked parent can set limits on the Shorts feed in Family Center, along with take a break and bedtime reminders and the content setting. Supervision is voluntary, and either the parent or the teen can turn it off.

### Can a teen skip a Shorts limit set by a parent?

YouTube's help doesn't say. The adult page says the limit can be dismissed or ignored, and the teen pages say only that parents can set it, so we could not confirm whether teens see a dismiss option.

### Does a supervised kid account have a Shorts limit?

YouTube's comparison page says parents can use Family Center to manage screen time limits for supervised kid accounts, including setting a Shorts feed limit. Making Shorts is among the features turned off for these accounts.

### How do time limits work in YouTube Kids?

YouTube Kids has a built-in timer. A parent opens Settings, solves a multiplication problem or enters a passcode, taps Set Timer and starts it. When time is up, kids see a Time's up! notification and the app locks.
