Snapchat has no setting to remove Spotlight. Turn off its notifications, hide Snaps you don't want, or hide Spotlight free in minulo and keep your chats.

## Short answers

### Can you turn off Spotlight on Snapchat?

We could not find a way in Snapchat's help as of October 2026. Spotlight stays in the tab bar at the bottom of the app. Snapchat's help says about Discover that you can't completely disable the section, and we found no setting for Spotlight either. To use Snapchat without Spotlight, open it in an app that hides it, such as minulo, where this is free.

### How do I turn off Spotlight notifications on Snapchat?

Tap your profile icon, tap the gear to open Settings, tap Notifications, and turn off each notification type you don't want. If the alerts are about comments on your own Spotlight Snaps, turn off Comments in the same Notifications screen. To stop every Snapchat notification, go to the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, tap Snapchat and turn off Allow Notifications.

### Can I make Snapchat open on a tab other than Spotlight?

Only with Snapchat+. Subscribers can open My Account, tap their Snapchat+ membership card, tap Home Tab and choose the tab the app opens on. Spotlight is still one tap away in the tab bar.

### Does hiding Snaps on Spotlight make it go away?

No. Snapchat says it treats hiding, skipping and reporting as signs that you don't enjoy that content and ranks it lower, but Spotlight keeps showing other Snaps. It works one Snap at a time.

### How do I hide Spotlight on Snapchat but keep my chats?

iPhone Screen Time limits the whole Snapchat app, chats included, so it does not help here. Use an app that opens Snapchat with Spotlight hidden. In minulo, hiding Spotlight and Discover is free, and chat opens straight from the home screen.
