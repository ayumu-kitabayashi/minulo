# How to do a one-week digital detox without unplugging completely

A one-week digital detox that keeps your messages: what to cut each day, what studies found when people quit Facebook or mobile internet, and what comes after.

## Short answers

### How do you do a digital detox for a week?

Keep calls, messages and the apps you need, and cut the feeds that take the most time. Write down your Screen Time before you start, hide or delete the feed apps, set Downtime for the evenings, plan what you will do instead, and compare your Screen Time at the end of the week.

### Can you do a digital detox without giving up your phone?

Yes. Removing the endless feeds and keeping messages is a detox most people can keep up. In a 2025 trial, people kept calls and texts and blocked only mobile internet for two weeks, and their attention and well-being improved.

### What are the benefits of a digital detox?

In a 2020 experiment, people who deactivated Facebook for four weeks reported higher well-being and used Facebook less afterwards. In a 2025 trial, two weeks without mobile internet improved sustained attention, mental health and well-being. Both studies used volunteers, and many found it hard to stick to the break.

### What should I do instead of scrolling during a digital detox?

Decide before you start. A walk, a book, cooking, exercise or seeing friends all work, as long as it is planned. In the 2020 Facebook study, people spent the time they got back watching TV and with friends and family.
