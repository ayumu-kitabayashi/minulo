# How to get Instagram notifications only for messages (iPhone, 2026)

We found no messages-only switch, but you can get close: turn off every other type in Instagram's Notifications, keep iPhone notifications on, mute busy chats.

## Short answers

### How do I get Instagram notifications only for messages?

Go to Profile › Menu › Notifications. Below Pause push notifications, open every notification type that isn't about messages and turn it off, and leave messages on. Keep Instagram allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications.

### Why am I not getting notifications for Instagram messages?

Check that Allow Notifications is on for Instagram in iPhone Settings › Notifications, that Pause push notifications and Sleep mode are off in Instagram, that the chat isn't muted, and that no blocker has locked the Instagram app.

### How do I mute one chat on Instagram?

Open the chat, tap the name at the top, tap Mute, then turn on Mute messages or Mute calls. The other person isn't told.

### Does minulo send Instagram DM notifications?

No. minulo sends no notifications, and while it locks the official Instagram app, that app's notifications don't arrive either. To get DM alerts, leave the official app unlocked.
