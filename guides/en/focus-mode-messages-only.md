# How to set up an iPhone Focus that allows only messaging apps (and what happens to Instagram DMs)

How to set up an iPhone Focus that lets only messaging apps notify you, and why Instagram DMs come through either with every Instagram alert or not at all.

## Short answers

### Can an iPhone Focus allow only messaging apps?

Yes. In Settings › Focus, choose a Focus, tap Apps, tap Allow Notifications From and add only Messages and your chat apps. Turn off Time Sensitive Notifications if you don't want other apps to break through.

### Will Instagram DMs come through a messages only Focus?

Only if Instagram is in the Focus's allowed apps. A Focus allows or silences an app as a whole, so allowing Instagram lets all of its notifications through. To keep just DMs, also turn off the other notification types inside Instagram.

### Does a Focus block Instagram?

No. A Focus filters notifications. You can still open Instagram while it is on. You can link a Home Screen page without Instagram to the Focus, and use Screen Time or an app blocker if you want the app itself blocked.

### What does Time Sensitive Notifications do in a Focus?

Apple says it lets all apps send time-sensitive notifications immediately. If you want only the apps you chose to reach you, turn it off on the Apps screen of that Focus.

### Does minulo send Instagram DM notifications during a Focus?

No. minulo sends no notifications. While minulo locks the official Instagram app, that app's notifications don't arrive either. For DM alerts, keep the official app unlocked and allowed in your Focus.
