# How to stop doomscrolling at night on iPhone (2026)

Stop doomscrolling at night on iPhone: Downtime with Block at Downtime, Sleep Focus, Grayscale, and minulo to hide Reels and Shorts for free while keeping DMs.

## Short answers

### How do I stop doomscrolling at night on iPhone?

If you want to keep your messages, open Instagram and YouTube in minulo, where Reels and Shorts are hidden for free, and lock the official apps at bedtime. If blocking DMs too is fine, schedule Downtime in Settings > Screen Time (Screen Time Schedule on iOS 27), set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime so it can't be ignored. Add a sleep schedule in the Health app so Sleep Focus starts during wind down, and charge your phone away from the bed.

### Does Sleep Focus block Instagram or TikTok?

No. Sleep Focus filters notifications and calls and can switch to a different Home Screen page, but it doesn't stop apps from opening. To stop apps, use Screen Time Downtime with Block at Downtime, or lock them with an app such as minulo.

### How do I make Downtime so I can't ignore it?

Set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime in Settings > Screen Time > Downtime. Blocked apps then appear dimmed with an hourglass and can't be opened. Also check Always Allowed, since apps listed there stay open.

### Can I block Instagram at night but still get DMs?

Not with Screen Time alone, because it blocks the whole app, DMs included. With minulo you can open Instagram with Reels hidden, or with only DMs left, and lock the official app at night. While the official app is locked, its notifications don't arrive.

### Is there a free way to stop scrolling before bed?

Yes. Downtime, Sleep Focus, Grayscale, YouTube's Shorts feed limit and Instagram's Sleep mode are all free. In minulo, hiding Reels and Shorts is free, and locking the official apps is free for up to 2 hours per block; automatic nightly schedules are Pro.
