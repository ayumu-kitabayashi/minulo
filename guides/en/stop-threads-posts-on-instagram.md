# How to stop seeing Threads posts on Instagram (iPhone, 2026)

No setting turns off Threads posts in your Instagram feed. Hide them one by one, switch to Following, or open Instagram without the feed.

## Short answers

### Why are Threads posts showing up in my Instagram feed?

Meta shows Threads posts directly on Instagram and Facebook. Instagram head Adam Mosseri announced the feature in November 2023 as a way to help Threads posts reach more people. They can come from accounts you don't follow.

### Can I turn off Threads posts on Instagram completely?

We could not find a setting for this in Instagram's help as of October 2026. You can hide Threads posts one at a time from their menu, or switch to the Following feed, which shows only accounts you follow. If you mainly use Instagram for DMs, minulo can open it with the whole feed hidden, for free.

### Does "Suggesting posts on other apps" stop Threads on Instagram?

Not for what you see. That Threads setting controls whether your own Threads posts are suggested on Instagram and Facebook. Other people's Threads posts can still appear in your Instagram feed.

### Does the Following feed remove Threads posts?

Following shows posts only from accounts you follow, with no suggested posts, so Threads posts that Instagram suggests from other accounts are not shown there. Instagram goes back to the regular feed when you reopen it, so you choose Following each time.

### How do I stop Threads notifications on Instagram?

Guiding Tech says you can swipe left on a Threads notification in Instagram's Notifications tab, tap the three dots and choose Turn off notifications like this. We could not find this step in Instagram's help.
