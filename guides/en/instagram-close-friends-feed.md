# Can you see only Close Friends or Favorites posts on Instagram? (iPhone, 2026)

Instagram has no Close Friends feed. Close Friends is a list for sharing stories. Favorites is the closest thing: up to 50 accounts, newest posts first.

## Short answers

### Can you see only Close Friends posts on Instagram?

No. As of October 2026, Instagram's help describes Close Friends as a list you share stories with, not a feed you can open. To see posts from a small group of people only, add them to Favorites and open the Favorites view from the top of your feed.

### How many people can I add to Instagram Favorites?

Meta says you can add up to 50 accounts. Removing someone from Favorites does not unfollow them.

### Does Favorites show posts in order?

Yes. Meta says both Favorites and Following show posts in chronological order, so recent posts are at the top.

### How do I know if I am on someone's Close Friends list?

Instagram shows a green ring around their profile photo and a Close Friends label when you view a story they shared to their Close Friends list.

### Does Favorites hide Reels?

No. Favorites only changes what the Home feed shows. The Reels tab and Explore stay in the app. minulo can hide the Reels and Explore tabs for free while your DMs keep working.
