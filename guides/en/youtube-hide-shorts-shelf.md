# Hiding the Shorts shelf on YouTube Home: how long it lasts and where Shorts stay (iPhone, 2026)

What the X on YouTube's Shorts shelf does on iPhone: the 30 days come from news reports, not YouTube Help; where Shorts stay; Show fewer Shorts; minulo hides the shelf for free.

## Short answers

### How do I hide the Shorts shelf on YouTube for good?

minulo hides the Shorts shelf and the Shorts tab for free and keeps them hidden until you change the setting, though only inside minulo. We found no permanent switch in YouTube's help; the X on the shelf hides it for 30 days, according to news reports.

### How long does the X hide YouTube Shorts?

30 days, according to SlashGear and GeekChamp, which quote the message YouTube shows. YouTube's help doesn't mention the X or a period, so treat the 30 days as reported, not official.

### Why is there no X on my Shorts shelf?

Reports say it isn't on every account. tbreak (April 2026) found it only on the mobile website, and GeekChamp (May 2026) says it depends on account, region and app version. In the app, tap More at the top of the Shorts grid and choose Show fewer Shorts.

### Where do Shorts still show after I hide the shelf?

In the Shorts tab, search results, Subscriptions, channel pages and notifications, which YouTube's help lists as places viewers find Shorts. The X only affects the shelf on Home.

### Does the Shorts feed limit remove the Shorts shelf?

YouTube's help describes it as a limit for the Shorts feed: at 0 minutes a limit screen appears, and adults can dismiss it. It doesn't say the shelf on Home goes away.
