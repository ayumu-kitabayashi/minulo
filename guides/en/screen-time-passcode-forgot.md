# Forgot the Screen Time passcode you set for yourself? Apple's official steps (iPhone, 2026)

Forgot the Screen Time passcode you set on your own iPhone? Apple's steps: Settings, Screen Time, Change Screen Time Passcode, then your Apple Account.

## Short answers

### What do I do if I forgot my Screen Time passcode?

Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then Manage Screen Time, and tap Change Screen Time Passcode twice. If asked, enter the Apple Account email and password you used to set up the passcode, then enter a new passcode twice.

### Can I reset my Screen Time passcode without my Apple Account?

Apple's page only describes resetting it with the Apple Account you entered for Screen Time Passcode Recovery. It doesn't give another way for your own device. For other cases, the page links to Apple Support.

### Why does my Apple Account not work for the Screen Time passcode reset?

You need the same Apple Account you used when you set up the Screen Time passcode. Apple says it may be different from the account signed in on your iPhone, or a family member's account if you are in Family Sharing.

### Is the Screen Time passcode the same as my iPhone passcode?

No. Apple says the Screen Time passcode isn't the passcode you use to unlock your device. It is only for changing Screen Time settings, limits and restrictions.

### Can minulo reset my Screen Time passcode?

No. minulo can't see or reset it. Use Apple's steps in Settings, Screen Time, Manage Screen Time, Change Screen Time Passcode.
