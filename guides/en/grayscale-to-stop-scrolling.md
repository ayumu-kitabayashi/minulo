# Does grayscale on iPhone reduce scrolling? How to set it up, and what it doesn't change (2026)

Apple describes grayscale as a Color Filters accessibility setting, not a screen time tool. How to turn it on, put it on a triple-click or Back Tap, and what it doesn't change.

## Short answers

### Does grayscale on iPhone reduce screen time?

Apple doesn't say so. It describes grayscale as a Color Filters accessibility setting, and we found no official source saying it reduces scrolling. Try it for a week and compare the app's time in Screen Time before and after.

### How do I turn on grayscale on iPhone?

Go to Settings, Accessibility, Display & Text Size, Color Filters. Turn on Color Filters and tap Grayscale. Apple notes that color filters can change the look of images and videos.

### How do I turn grayscale on and off quickly?

Use the Accessibility Shortcut in Settings, Accessibility, Accessibility Shortcut, then triple-click the side or Home button. If Color Filters is the only feature you pick, the triple-click switches it right away. Back Tap in Settings, Accessibility, Touch, Back Tap can also turn on an accessibility feature.

### Can grayscale turn on automatically when I open Instagram?

Apple's Shortcuts guide has an App trigger that runs an automation when you open a chosen app. Apple's guide doesn't name an action for color filters, so we could not confirm that the full setup works. Check the action list in Shortcuts on your iPhone.

### Does Night Shift turn off grayscale?

No. Apple says Night Shift automatically removes inverted colors and color filter effects, except grayscale.
