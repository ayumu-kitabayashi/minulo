# How to stop checking Instagram and TikTok first thing in the morning (iPhone, 2026)

Keep Reels and TikTok off in the morning on iPhone: a Downtime schedule that runs past wake-up, Sleep Focus, and minulo to hide Reels but keep DMs.

## Short answers

### Why do I check my phone as soon as I wake up?

For many people the phone is the alarm, so it is already in hand, and the apps on the home screen open straight to a feed. Moving the alarm and hiding the feed both cut that first tap.

### Can iPhone block Instagram until a set time in the morning?

Yes. A Downtime schedule with Block at Downtime on blocks apps outside Always Allowed until the end time you set.

### Does Sleep Focus stop me opening Instagram?

No. A Focus filters notifications. To stop apps opening, use Downtime with Block at Downtime, or lock the apps with minulo.

### Can I still read messages in the morning?

Yes. Keep Messages in Always Allowed. For Instagram DMs, open Instagram inside minulo, where Reels are hidden. The official app's notifications don't arrive while minulo blocks it.

### Is there a free way to do this?

Downtime and Sleep Focus are built into iPhone. minulo hides Reels and For You for free and locks the official apps for free, up to 2 hours per block.
