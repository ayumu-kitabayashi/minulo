# How to block Instagram, TikTok or YouTube for one hour on iPhone (2026)

iPhone has no one-hour block: App Limits count daily use and Downtime turned on by hand runs until tomorrow. minulo locks apps free, up to 2 hours per block.

## Short answers

### Can I block Instagram for just one hour on iPhone?

Yes, with minulo, which locks the official Instagram app with Screen Time for free, up to 2 hours per block. iPhone's own Screen Time has no one-hour button: App Limits count daily use, and Downtime turned on by hand lasts until tomorrow.

### Can I set Screen Time to block an app for 25 minutes?

Not as a one-off timer. App Limits set a daily amount, and Downtime runs on a schedule or until tomorrow. You can schedule a Downtime window for your study time, but you set it in advance in Settings.

### Is there a Pomodoro app that blocks Instagram?

Several focus timer apps say they block apps during a session. We haven't tested them, so check their App Store pages. minulo has no timer of its own, but its free block keeps the official apps locked for up to 2 hours, which covers a few 25-minute rounds.

### Can I still reply to messages while Instagram is blocked?

With minulo, yes. Open Instagram inside minulo with Reels hidden and use your DMs there. The official app's notifications don't arrive while it is blocked.

### What if I really need the app during the block?

In minulo, tap Take a break and choose the minutes. The official app opens for that long, and the block turns back on by itself when time is up.

### Can the block start by itself every day?

With Downtime, yes, on the schedule you set. In minulo, schedules by day and time are part of minulo Pro. On the free plan you start each block yourself.
