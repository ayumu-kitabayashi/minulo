# Can you turn off Reels and the video feed on Facebook? (iPhone, 2026)

Facebook has no setting that turns Reels off, and since September 2025 every video on Facebook is a reel. What you can do: Not Interested, Hide reel and autoplay off.

## Short answers

### Can you turn off Reels on Facebook?

Not with a setting, as far as Facebook's help shows in October 2026. Since September 2025 every video on Facebook is a reel. You can tap More on a reel and choose Not Interested or Hide reel, which makes similar reels show up less over time.

### How do I see fewer reels on Facebook?

While watching a reel, tap More and choose Not Interested, or tap Hide reel to remove it and reduce content from that creator. Facebook says the effect builds gradually and fades over time.

### How do I stop Facebook videos from playing automatically on iPhone?

Tap Menu, then Settings & Privacy, then Settings. Under Preferences, tap Media, and under Autoplay choose Never Autoplay Videos. Reels still appear, but they don't start by themselves.

### Why do I see reels from people I don't follow?

Facebook says reels are recommended based on what may be relevant to you, using signals such as what you watch, like and share, what is trending, your location and the time of day. That is why they show up in your Feed and in Video.

### Can I use Facebook without Reels and keep my messages?

Not in the official app. In minulo, Facebook opens with Reels and Watch hidden, and Notifications, Messages, Friends and Feed open from the minulo home screen. Hiding is free.
