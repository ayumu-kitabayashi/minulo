# How to get rid of the For you tab on X (iPhone, 2026)

X has no setting that removes For you, but it reopens on the tab you used last. How to stay on Following, mute words, and hide For you.

## Short answers

### Can I turn off the For you tab on X?

We could not find a setting that removes it in X's help as of October 2026. You can switch to Following, and X reopens on the timeline you had open last when you closed it. To have the For you tab hidden every time, open X in an app that hides it, such as minulo, where this is free.

### How do I make X open to Following instead of For you?

Switch to Following at the top of Home and close X while you are still on it. X's help says the app reopens on whichever timeline you had open last. If you look at For you before closing, it will open on For you next time.

### Why does X keep going back to For you?

X remembers the tab you were on when you closed the app. If you swiped over to For you, even briefly, that is the tab you get next time. X may also show recommended posts in notifications, which can take you back into recommended content.

### Does muting words remove posts from For you?

Muted words apply to your Home timeline and notifications, so posts containing them are kept out of Home. Muted posts still appear in search, and notifications you turned on for a specific account are not muted. You add entries one at a time and can mute them forever or for 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days.

### Can I use X just for DMs?

X has no setting for that. Screen Time blocks the whole app, messages included. In minulo, X opens to Messages with the For you tab hidden, and Search, Video and Grok can be hidden too. Hiding is free.
