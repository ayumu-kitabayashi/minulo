# How to use Instagram DMs on a computer, or without opening the app (2026)

instagram.com has DMs in a computer browser, but Reels and Explore are there too. How to read DMs without the feed, and minulo for DMs only, free on iPhone.

## Short answers

### Can I use Instagram only for DMs without the app?

Yes: minulo opens Instagram's website with only DMs and the notifications tab left, free with its Every tab but DMs setting, though only inside minulo on iPhone. On a computer, instagram.com has DMs too, but the Reels and Explore tabs are still there.

### Can you use Instagram DMs on a computer?

Yes. Open instagram.com in a browser, log in and click Messages on the left. About Instagram describes Compose, choosing people, Chat and Send. We couldn't confirm in Instagram Help which message features the computer website has.

### Does instagram.com on a computer have Reels?

Yes. The website has the Reels and Explore tabs too, so moving to a computer doesn't make Instagram DMs only.

### Can I read Instagram DMs without opening the app?

On iPhone, go to Settings, Notifications, Instagram, and set Show Previews to Always. Apple says not all apps support previews, and we couldn't confirm what Instagram's DM alerts show. Tapping the alert opens the chat in the app.

### Is there a minulo for Mac?

Yes. minulo runs on iPhone and Mac, plus Chrome and Safari extensions. Its privacy policy says the Mac app reads only the address of the page you have open and sends ways into a feed or short videos to a place you choose. This page doesn't cover which Instagram parts each version hides.

### Will I still get DM notifications if I use minulo?

Yes, from the official Instagram app, as long as minulo isn't locking it. While minulo locks the official app, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own.
