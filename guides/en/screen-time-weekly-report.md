# How to read the iPhone Screen Time weekly report and pickups to find your scrolling time (2026)

Where the Screen Time weekly report is on iOS 27 and iOS 26, what Week, Pickups and Notifications show, and how to find your scrolling time.

## Short answers

### Where is the Screen Time weekly report on iPhone?

On iOS 27, open Settings, tap Screen Time, tap Daily Average and then Week. On iOS 26, tap Screen Time, then See All App & Website Activity, then Week. You can also tap the Screen Time Weekly Report notification, which stays in Notification Center if you miss it.

### What do Pickups mean in Screen Time?

Apple says Pickups shows how many times you picked up your devices. You find it by scrolling down in the Screen Time summary, next to Notifications.

### What day does the Screen Time weekly report come?

Apple's iPhone User Guide doesn't give a day. It says the report appears as a notification and that you can find it in Notification Center if it disappears. You can open the weekly summary in Settings at any time.

### Can Screen Time show how long I spent on Reels or Shorts?

No. The report shows time per app and website, so Reels, DMs and posting all count as Instagram time. To use Instagram without Reels, open it in an app that hides them, such as minulo, where hiding is free.

### Does the weekly report include my iPad and Mac?

Yes, if Share Across Devices is on in Manage Screen Time. Apple says it combines Screen Time data from devices signed in to the same Apple Account. On iOS 26 you can pick one device with Devices.
