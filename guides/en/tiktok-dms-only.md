# How to use TikTok for DMs only on iPhone, without the For You feed (2026)

TikTok opens on For You and has no messages-only mode. How to keep TikTok messages on iPhone while hiding For You and the other feeds.

## Short answers

### Does TikTok have a messages-only mode?

Not that we could find. TikTok has a Following tab, but the app still opens on For You. minulo hides For You and can show only your messages.

### Will my friends still be able to message me?

Yes. Messages keep working in minulo; only the feeds are hidden.

### Can I do the same for Instagram?

Yes. Instagram has the same "Every tab but DMs" setting in minulo.
