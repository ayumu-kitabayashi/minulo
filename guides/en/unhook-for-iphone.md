### How do I hide YouTube recommendations on iPhone?

Open YouTube in minulo and turn on Home recommendations, or Everything on home but search. In YouTube itself, Google's help says that deleting and turning off watch history keeps video recommendations off Home.

### Can I hide YouTube comments on iPhone?

Yes, inside minulo: Comments is one of the YouTube items you can hide, for free. The comment settings in YouTube Help are for creators managing their own videos.

### Does minulo change the official YouTube app?

No. Shorts and recommendations are hidden only when you open YouTube inside minulo. To keep yourself out of the official app, lock it in minulo's Block tab, free for up to 2 hours per block.

### Is minulo free?

Hiding Shorts, Home recommendations and comments is free with no time limit. Schedules, blocks longer than 2 hours and Hard Mode are in minulo Pro.
