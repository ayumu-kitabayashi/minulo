# Screen Time Downtime not blocking Instagram on iPhone? 5 causes (2026)

Downtime lets you keep using Instagram? Check Block at Downtime, the Screen Time passcode, Always Allowed, the schedule, and iOS 27 changes.

## Short answers

### Why does Downtime not block Instagram?

Usually because Block at Downtime is off, so Downtime only shows a reminder and lets you continue. Turn it on under Screen Time, Downtime. It needs a Screen Time passcode. Also check that Instagram is not in Always Allowed.

### Why can I still open apps during Downtime?

Apps in Always Allowed stay available during downtime. Without Block at Downtime, other apps show a reminder that you can skip. With Block at Downtime on, restricted apps are dimmed with an hourglass and cannot be opened.

### Where is Block at Downtime?

Settings, Screen Time, Downtime. Apple says you can choose to block apps completely during downtime once you have set a Screen Time passcode, so set one first with Lock Screen Time Settings.

### Where did Downtime go in iOS 27?

In Apple's iOS 27 guide, the schedules page covers Screen Time Schedules and Time Allowances instead of Downtime. Go to Screen Time, Screen Time Schedule, and turn on Schedule. These need iOS 27 or later on all your Apple devices.

### Can Downtime block only Instagram Reels?

No. Downtime works on whole apps, so DMs are blocked too. To hide only Reels, open Instagram in an app that hides them. In minulo this is free and DMs keep working.
