# How to reset your Instagram Reels recommendations on iPhone (2026)

Reset suggested content in Instagram's Content preferences to clear your Reels, Explore and Feed recommendations. What it changes, why the same Reels come back, and how to see fewer Reels at all.

## Short answers

### Can you reset Instagram Reels recommendations?

Yes. Go to your profile, tap the menu, then Content preferences and Reset suggested content. It clears your recommendations across Explore, Reels and Feed at the same time. There is no option in Instagram's help to reset Reels only.

### How do I reset my Instagram algorithm?

Use Reset suggested content under Content preferences in Instagram's settings. Instagram's help says it cannot be undone, and your suggestions become personalized again as you like, share and interact with content.

### Will resetting unfollow anyone or delete my data?

No. Instagram's help says resetting suggested content does not change who you follow or the ads you see, and does not delete your data. During the reset you can choose to review your following list and unfollow accounts yourself.

### Why do the same Reels come back after a reset?

Because recommendations learn again from what you interact with after the reset. If you watch and like the same kinds of Reels, you will see more of them again. Tap Not interested on what you don't want, or add words with Don't suggest posts with certain words.

### How do I see fewer Reels on Instagram?

Instagram has no setting for adults that turns Reels off. Tapping the Instagram logo and choosing Following removes suggested posts from your feed, but the Reels tab stays. To hide Reels, open Instagram in an app that hides them. In minulo this is free and DMs keep working.
