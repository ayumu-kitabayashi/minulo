# SocialLite vs UNDOOMED: two apps that hide Reels and keep your DMs (2026)

SocialLite vs UNDOOMED compared: networks, messages-only mode, notifications, Android, prices and ratings. Neither lists TikTok; where minulo fits.

## Short answers

### Which is better, SocialLite or UNDOOMED?

It depends on what you need. SocialLite has far more ratings (15,412 in the US, average 4.7) and push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat. UNDOOMED runs on Android as well as iPhone, has a Messages Only mode for Instagram and LinkedIn, and its paid plans cost less.

### Do SocialLite or UNDOOMED work with TikTok?

Neither lists it. SocialLite's App Store description and release notes don't mention TikTok, and UNDOOMED lists TikTok and Snapchat as unavailable. minulo opens TikTok with For You, Explore and Create hidden while messages keep working, for free.

### Is SocialLite or UNDOOMED on Android?

UNDOOMED is on iPhone and Android. The sources we used describe SocialLite on iPhone and iPad only. minulo is not on Android yet.

### Which one is cheaper?

UNDOOMED. Its Plus plan is $1.99 a month and Pro is $2.99 a month, $17.99 a year or $29.99 lifetime in the US. SocialLite's Pro costs $6.99 to $17.99 a month or $19.99 to $47.90 a year, depending on the offer shown (US, checked 2026-10-26). Both are free to download.

### Which one collects less data?

On their US App Store privacy labels (checked 2026-10-23), SocialLite lists Identifiers under Data Used to Track You, and UNDOOMED lists nothing there. UNDOOMED's Data Not Linked to You is a device ID, product interaction and crash data. Apple notes that developers write these labels and Apple does not verify them.
