# Is there News Feed Eradicator for iPhone? Hiding social media feeds and keeping messages (2026)

News Feed Eradicator is a Chrome extension. On iPhone, minulo hides the Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube feeds while messages keep working, and hiding is free.

## Short answers

### Is News Feed Eradicator available on iPhone?

Its Chrome Web Store listing describes a Chrome extension and doesn't mention iPhone or Safari. On iPhone, minulo hides the Facebook home feed, the LinkedIn news feed and every Instagram tab but DMs, and hiding is free.

### Can I hide the Facebook feed on iPhone and keep messages?

Yes, inside minulo. Open Facebook there and turn on The home feed. Your messages and notifications stay, and Reels and Watch and Marketplace can be hidden too.

### Can I hide the LinkedIn feed and still use messages?

Yes. In minulo, turn on The news feed for LinkedIn, and keep using it for messages and jobs.

### Does minulo show a quote like News Feed Eradicator?

No. minulo simply hides the parts you switch on.

### Does it work inside the official apps?

No. Hidden parts are hidden only inside minulo. To stop yourself from opening the official apps, lock them in the Block tab, free for up to 2 hours per block.

### Is minulo free?

Hiding is free on all 10 networks with no time limit. Sleep mode, longer blocks, Hard Mode and a daily limit are in minulo Pro.
