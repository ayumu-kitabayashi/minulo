# Is SocialLite free? Its price, and apps that hide Reels for free (2026)

SocialLite is free to download with in-app purchases; in the US, Pro costs $6.99 to $17.99 a month or $19.99 to $47.90 a year (checked 2026-10-26). What is free in similar apps, and where minulo fits.

## Short answers

### Is SocialLite free?

It is free to download, with in-app purchases. Its App Store description doesn't say which features are free and which need Pro.

### How much is SocialLite Pro?

On the US App Store (checked 2026-10-26), Pro costs $6.99 to $17.99 a month or $19.99 to $47.90 a year, depending on the offer shown, and a Family plan is $12.99 a month. For comparison, minulo Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

### Is there a free app like SocialLite?

Nope is completely free but only works in Safari on instagram.com and youtube.com. Among apps that work like SocialLite, minulo, Stillfeed, Reelmute and Awhile hide Reels for free and charge only for extras. minulo also has a free messages-only setting for Instagram and TikTok.

### Can I get notifications with a free option?

In minulo, yes, as long as you leave the official app unblocked: notifications come from the official app as usual and you can reply inside minulo. While minulo blocks the official app, its notifications don't arrive. SocialLite has its own push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat.

### What does minulo Pro add?

Schedules by day and time, blocks longer than 2 hours, Hard Mode, blocking a network completely and multiple block lists. Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts stays free with no time limit.
