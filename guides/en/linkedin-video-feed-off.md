# Can you turn off the LinkedIn video feed and autoplay on iPhone? (2026)

LinkedIn lets you stop videos from autoplaying, but has no setting to remove the Video tab or the full-screen vertical video feed. What you can and can’t change.

## Short answers

### Can you turn off the video feed on LinkedIn?

We found no setting for it. LinkedIn's help says there are no options to customize the full-screen vertical video feed, and the Video tab stays in the navigation bar for members who have it. minulo opens LinkedIn's website with the video feed and games hidden, for free.

### How do I stop videos autoplaying on LinkedIn on iPhone?

Tap your profile photo, then Settings & Privacy, then Account preferences. Under General Preferences tap Autoplay Videos and choose Never autoplay videos.

### Why do short LinkedIn videos keep looping?

LinkedIn says there is currently no on/off toggle for auto-looping on videos under 30 seconds.

### Why don't I have the Video tab on LinkedIn?

LinkedIn says the full-screen video feed, the video tab and the video carousel may not be available to all members. They are also not on the desktop site.

### Does turning off autoplay remove videos from my LinkedIn feed?

LinkedIn describes the setting as controlling whether videos play automatically as you scroll. It does not say the setting hides video posts, and the Video tab is a separate feature with no off switch.
