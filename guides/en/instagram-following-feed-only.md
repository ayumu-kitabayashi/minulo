# How to see only people you follow on Instagram (Following feed, 2026)

Tap Instagram at the top left and choose Following to see only accounts you follow. Why it doesn't stay, how Favorites works, and how to hide Reels too.

## Short answers

### How do I see only posts from people I follow on Instagram?

Tap Instagram at the top left of your feed, then tap Following. Instagram's help says this shows posts from people you follow, with newer posts at the top. Meta says there are no suggested posts in this view and you can see the last 30 days.

### Can I make Instagram open to the Following feed by default?

We could not find a setting for this in Instagram's help as of October 2026. Instagram's help on Feed says that when you close and reopen Instagram or refresh your feed, the posts it predicts are most relevant to you appear toward the top. Plan on choosing Following again when you come back.

### What is the difference between Following and Favorites?

Following shows posts from everyone you follow. Favorites shows only the accounts you add to your Favorites list, up to 50, and their posts also appear higher in your main feed. Both are in time order and open from the Instagram menu at the top of your feed.

### Does the Following feed get rid of Reels and Explore?

No. Following changes your feed only. The Reels tab and Explore still show recommendations from accounts you don't follow. To hide them, open Instagram in an app that hides Reels and Explore, such as minulo, where this is free.

### How do I use Instagram without the algorithm?

Instagram has no switch that turns recommendations off everywhere. The Following and Favorites feeds are in time order, but Reels and Explore are still ranked for you. In minulo you can hide suggested posts, Reels and Explore for free, so what is left is your DMs, posts from people you follow and search.
