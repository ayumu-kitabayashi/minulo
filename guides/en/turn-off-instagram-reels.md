## Short answers

### Is there a setting to turn off Reels on Instagram?

We could not find one in Instagram's official help pages as of September 2026. The Following feed removes suggested posts from your feed, but the Reels tab stays.

### Can Screen Time block only Instagram Reels?

No. Screen Time App Limits work on the whole app, and website blocking works on whole addresses. Blocking Instagram this way also blocks your DMs.

### Can I use Instagram without Reels?

Yes, but not inside the official app, because it has no switch for adults to turn Reels off.
