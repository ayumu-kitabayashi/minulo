# How to use YouTube with only your subscriptions on iPhone (2026)

The YouTube app opens on Home, and Subs is a feed in its top bar. Pausing history empties Home but hides that bar too. What the app lets you do.

## Short answers

### Can I make the YouTube app open on Subscriptions?

We could not find a setting for that in YouTube's help as of October 2026. The help says the app opens on the Home tab, and Subs is a feed you tap in the top bar of Home.

### Where is the Subscriptions tab in the YouTube app?

YouTube's help for iPhone describes it as the Subs feed in the top bar of the Home tab. It shows the newest videos from channels you subscribe to and a list of your channels at the top.

### Why did Subs disappear from the top bar?

YouTube's help on feeds says the feeds and the top bar are not visible if your watch history is paused or turned off. The help page points you to turning watch history back on.

### Can I hide YouTube recommendations and keep subscriptions?

Not with a YouTube setting we could find that keeps Subs in place. In minulo, YouTube opens on Subscriptions with Home recommendations and Shorts hidden, and it's free.

### Does minulo change the official YouTube app?

No. minulo shows YouTube's website inside its own app. It can lock the official app with Screen Time, and while it is locked, the official app's notifications don't arrive.
