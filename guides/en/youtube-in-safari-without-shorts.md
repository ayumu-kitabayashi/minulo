# Can you use YouTube in Safari without Shorts on iPhone? (2026)

Using youtube.com in Safari instead of the app doesn't remove Shorts. What works on iPhone: a Safari extension such as Nope, the X on the Shorts shelf, history off, and minulo for free.

## Short answers

### Can you watch YouTube in Safari without Shorts on iPhone?

Yes, with help: minulo opens YouTube's website with the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab hidden for free while normal videos, search and subscriptions keep working, though only inside minulo. In plain Safari, Shorts stay unless you add an extension such as Nope.

### Does the Shorts feed limit work in Safari?

YouTube's help gives the steps for the YouTube app only and doesn't say whether the limit applies on the website. At 0 minutes, adults can dismiss the reminder anyway.

### Is there a free Safari extension that hides YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Nope is free with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads, per its App Store page, and hides Shorts in the sidebar, the Home shelf, the search chip and channel pages on youtube.com. It works only in Safari.

### Should I delete the YouTube app to stop watching Shorts?

Deleting removes the app and its data from your iPhone, but youtube.com still shows Shorts in Safari and the app is back in a minute. Locking the app instead keeps it installed: minulo does this with Screen Time, free for up to 2 hours per block.

### Does Request Desktop Site remove YouTube Shorts?

We couldn't confirm that in YouTube Help. On a computer, YouTube Home has a Shorts shelf too, and the help only describes marking it Not interested, which is feedback with no period.

### Can minulo hide Shorts in the official YouTube app?

No. On iPhone no app can change what another app shows. minulo hides Shorts in its own view of YouTube and can lock the official app with Screen Time. While it is locked, YouTube notifications don't arrive.
