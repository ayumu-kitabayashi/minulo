# How to lock Instagram, TikTok and YouTube automatically at night on iPhone (2026)

Downtime with Block at Downtime stops apps at set times. Sleep Focus, Require Face ID and Shortcuts don't block them. minulo locks apps for free, nightly with Pro.

## Short answers

### Can I make Instagram lock automatically at night on iPhone?

Yes: minulo locks the official Instagram app with Screen Time for free, up to 2 hours per block, and minulo Pro starts the block every night on a schedule. Built in, Downtime with Block at Downtime stops every app that is not Always Allowed at the times you set.

### Does Sleep Focus block apps?

Apple describes Sleep Focus as filtering notifications and calls and showing that you're unavailable. Its guide doesn't say a Focus stops an app from opening. You can switch to a Home Screen page without Instagram while it's on.

### Can a Shortcuts automation close or lock an app at a set time?

Automations can run at a time of day, but we couldn't find an action in Apple's Shortcuts guide that closes, locks or blocks another app. Use Downtime or a blocker app for the lock.

### Does Require Face ID stop me from opening Instagram at night?

No. It asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time you open the app, so you can still open it yourself, and it is not tied to a time of night.

### Can I lock Instagram at night and keep my messaging apps?

Yes. With Downtime, add the messaging apps to Always Allowed and leave Instagram out. minulo locks only the apps you choose and leaves the rest open.

### Is there a free way to lock apps automatically every night?

Downtime is free and built in. minulo's locks are free for up to 2 hours per block that you start, but a nightly schedule needs minulo Pro.
