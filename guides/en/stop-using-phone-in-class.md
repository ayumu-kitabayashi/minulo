# How to stop checking Instagram and TikTok in class on iPhone (2026)

A Focus can switch on at class time and hide Instagram from the Home Screen, but it does not block it. minulo locks apps free for up to 2 hours, about one class.

## Short answers

### How do I stop checking Instagram in class?

Lock the official Instagram app with minulo for free, up to 2 hours per block, so it won't open during the class. With minulo Pro the block can start by itself on your class days, and built in, Downtime with Block at Downtime does the same on a schedule.

### Does Focus mode block apps during class?

Apple describes a Focus as silencing notifications, or allowing only some. Its guide doesn't say a Focus stops apps from opening, but it can switch to a Home Screen page without them.

### Can a Focus turn on by itself when I get to class?

Yes. Apple says a Focus can turn on automatically at a set time, at a location or while using a certain app. Tap Add Schedule in that Focus.

### Can I block Instagram in class but keep the class group chat?

Yes. Open Instagram inside minulo and turn on Every tab but DMs, and group chats keep working there. With Downtime, a group chat in another app stays open if that app is in Always Allowed.

### Can I set different block times for different class days?

With Downtime, choose Customize Days and set the times for each day. In minulo, schedules by day and time are part of minulo Pro.

### Will I miss notifications during class?

While minulo blocks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. With a Focus, the people and apps you allow can still notify you.
