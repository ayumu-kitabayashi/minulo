# minulo vs Stillfeed: two iPhone apps that hide Reels and Shorts and keep your DMs (2026)

Stillfeed and minulo both hide Reels and Shorts and keep DMs. Stillfeed covers 7 networks with DMs Only in Pro; minulo covers 10, and its messages-only view is free.

## Short answers

### Which one should a student who needs Instagram for group chats use?

Both keep Instagram DMs while Reels are hidden, and that part is free in both. If you want Instagram to show nothing but messages, Stillfeed's DMs Only is part of Pro, while minulo's Every tab but DMs setting is free. If every network you use is among Stillfeed's 7 and you don't need a messages-only view, Stillfeed covers what you need.

### Does Stillfeed work with TikTok?

Yes. Since version 1.2.3 (October 27), its App Store description includes TikTok: For You, Discover, LIVE and mini dramas send you to Following. In minulo, TikTok's For You feed is hidden and messages keep working, and there is also a free DMs-only setting for TikTok.

### What happens when I tap the real Instagram icon?

Stillfeed says its blocking happens inside its own browser, not inside Instagram's app, and locking the official app is not mentioned on its App Store page, so the official app opens as usual. minulo can lock the official app with Screen Time; tapping the icon shows a block screen with an Open in minulo button that leads to Instagram without Reels.

### Which one is more established?

Both are new. Stillfeed has been on the App Store since July 2026 and minulo since September 2026. Neither had any US App Store ratings on 2026-10-27.
