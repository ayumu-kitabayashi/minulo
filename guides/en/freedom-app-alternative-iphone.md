# Freedom app alternative for iPhone that hides Reels but keeps your DMs (2026)

Freedom blocks whole apps and websites during a session. minulo hides Reels and Shorts so Instagram DMs still work, and hiding is free.

## Short answers

### Is there a free alternative to Freedom on iPhone?

Apple's Screen Time is built in: App Limits set a daily amount and Downtime blocks apps on a schedule. minulo is free to download, hides Reels and Shorts for free and locks the official apps for free, up to 2 hours per block.

### Does Freedom block Instagram DMs?

Freedom blocks the apps on your blocklist during a session, so Instagram is blocked as a whole, DMs included. minulo hides Reels and keeps DMs working inside its own view.

### How does Freedom block apps on iPhone?

Freedom's support site says it uses Apple's built-in Screen Time controls for apps and a local VPN profile for websites.

### Can minulo block websites like Freedom?

No. minulo hides Reels, Shorts and other feeds inside its own view and can lock the official apps. It does not block websites in Safari or other browsers.

### How much does Freedom cost?

The US App Store page lists Freedom as free to download with subscriptions, and its description says a 7-day free trial, then $39.99 a year. Check the page for current prices.
