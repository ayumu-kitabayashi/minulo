# How to stop videos on Pinterest: turn off autoplay and hide video Pins (iPhone, 2026)

Pinterest lets you turn off autoplay, but in the app the switch is named for cellular data. How to change it, and how to hide video Pins from your feed.

## Short answers

### How do I turn off autoplay videos on Pinterest?

In the Pinterest app, tap your profile picture at the bottom right, tap the settings (nut) icon at the top right, tap Social permissions and switch off Autoplay videos on cellular data. On a computer, open Settings, then Social permissions, switch off Autoplay videos on desktop and click Save.

### Why do Pinterest videos still play on Wi-Fi?

The app switch in Pinterest's help is named Autoplay videos on cellular data, and the help page says nothing about Wi-Fi. We could not confirm from Pinterest's help that videos stop playing on their own over Wi-Fi. Pinterest says video Pins play once 50% of the Pin is in view.

### Can I stop seeing video Pins on Pinterest altogether?

We could not find a setting for that in Pinterest's help as of October 2026. Pinterest says video Pins appear in your home feed and search results, and its settings only control autoplay. In minulo you can turn on Video pins to hide them, and keep your saved Pins and messages. That is free.

### Does Refine your recommendations remove videos?

Not as described in Pinterest's help. Refine your recommendations has two tabs: AI content, where you can see fewer AI-generated Pins by topic, and Activity, where you can turn off recommendations based on Pins you engaged with. Neither mentions video Pins.

### How can I use Pinterest just for my saved Pins?

Pinterest's home feed is built from recommendations based on your saves, searches and browsing. In minulo, the Recommendations switch hides the home feed along with Today, Videos and Ideas, and Saved and Messages open straight from the minulo home screen. You can hide the search box too.
