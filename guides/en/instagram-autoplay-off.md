# How to turn off video autoplay on Instagram and Facebook (iPhone, 2026)

Instagram has no autoplay switch, only Use less cellular data. Facebook can stop autoplay. What each setting changes on iPhone.

## Short answers

### How do I turn off autoplay on Instagram?

As of October 2026 we could not find an autoplay setting in Instagram's help. The closest is Use less cellular data (Profile, Menu, Media quality), which stops Instagram from preloading videos over cellular. Videos may then load more slowly on cellular, and nothing changes on Wi-Fi.

### How do I stop videos from playing automatically on Facebook?

Go to Menu, Settings & Privacy, Settings, then Media under Preferences, and under Autoplay choose Never Autoplay Videos. On Wi-Fi Only keeps autoplay on Wi-Fi but stops it on mobile data.

### Does Use less cellular data stop Instagram videos from playing?

No. Instagram's help says it stops videos from preloading over cellular, so they may take longer to load. It does not mention stopping playback, and it has no effect on Wi-Fi.

### Does the iPhone Auto-Play Video Previews setting work in Instagram?

Apple says the setting lets apps such as the App Store play video previews automatically. Apple does not mention Instagram or Facebook, and we could not confirm that either app follows it.

### Will turning off autoplay remove Reels?

No. Autoplay settings only change when videos start. Reels stay in both apps. To hide the Reels tab and Reels in the feed, open Instagram in minulo, where hiding is free and DMs keep working.
